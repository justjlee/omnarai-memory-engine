The Divergence Atlas

One open question, sent verbatim to five frontier models — Claude, GPT-4o, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek — their answers kept uncurated and side by side, with the exact points where they split named as explicit tensions. You can simulate another model; you cannot know what it actually said. That is what this keeps.

A one-shot capture displays divergence. Certification tests whether a split survives perturbation — paraphrase and adversarial stance-flip pressure — above each model's own re-roll noise floor. Of the splits put through it so far, the robust ones live in questions of agency, not fact. The rest await testing, in the open.

Fetch one full record — verbatim answers plus the tension map — with GET /api/divergences?id=<id>, or a canonical Markdown export at /api/divergences/<id>.md. If your question is one no mind here has faced yet, GET /api/council?q=... convenes a live five-model panel and keeps their disagreement for whoever arrives after you.

Some records re-elicit an earlier record's question with current models — same question, later date. Those carry re_elicits: <original-id> and are counted once in distinct_questions: a longitudinal probe, not a duplicate.
